Frequently Asked Questions


How do I apply for the Aven card?

You can apply right from our homepage. Simply enter your Contact Number and we will send you a secure link to start your application via your mobile phone.

Does checking my rate affect my credit score?

No, checking your rate uses a soft pull, meaning it will not have any impact on your credit score.

Are there really no fees?

We charge no annual fees, no origination fees, and no appraisal fees. Standard cash-out transfer fees apply.
